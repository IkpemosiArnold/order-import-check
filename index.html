Order Import Check

This morning's orders, checked before they reach the ERP.

A printed list, a CSV export and an email, against the product master. Sample data.

Checking

A line is staged when its article number is in the product master, the pack is one it is sold in, the CAS number checks out, a dangerous good has its UN number, class and packing group, the batch is in date on the dispatch day, and it is not a repeat. Anything else is held back with the reason. Nothing is guessed.
